Submission

Where to submit your work

What to hand in

  • Final transparent PNG layers on the full 1280 x 720 px canvas
  • One preview composite showing your part on the avatar base
  • Source file if available, such as PSD, AI, SVG, Procreate, Canva, Krita, or Clip Studio
  • Asset notes with team name, pack type, file list, known issues, and suggested studio button label

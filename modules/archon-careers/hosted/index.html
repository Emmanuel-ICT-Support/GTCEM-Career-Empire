How to play
  1. The adjudicator reserves a Game ID. Two players join with that code.
  2. On your turn, select one of your pieces and choose a highlighted square. Drag the board to change your view.
  3. Challenge an opposing piece to start an Initiative duel. Both players answer privately, then discuss the revealed answers using the Initiative guide.
  4. The adjudicator records the verdict and explains why. After a decisive duel, the winner chooses an available territory effect when prompted. Select a marked square to read its rule.
  5. A side wins when its opponent has no legal moves. If neither side can move, the match is drawn.

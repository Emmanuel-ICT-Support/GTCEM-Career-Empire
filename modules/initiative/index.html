What is this?

The skill of moving first, wisely.

Initiative means noticing what needs doing and taking the first useful, safe step before someone has to chase you.

Why should you care?

It changes how people read you.

Trust, reputation, responsibility, better tasks, stronger references, and real career options often start with a small action nobody forced you to take.

Warning

Initiative can go wrong.

Rushing, taking over, ignoring instructions, or trying to control everything is not smart initiative. The skill is knowing when, why, and how to act.

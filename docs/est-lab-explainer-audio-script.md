# EST Lab Explainer Audio Script

## Production Shape

Use the EST Lab explainer as a first-watch video with voiceover, then let students either replay the video or switch to a scrollable slide deck. The slide deck should use the same six beats so students can review at their own pace without learning a second version of the explanation.

The local Remotion timing has been stretched to a 60-second structure. Re-render `Assets/EST Preparation/est-lab-systems.mp4` before publishing the final audio version.

Audio bed direction: low-pressure synth pulse, light percussion, 95-105 BPM, no lyrics, loopable under speech. Duck the music under the voiceover, then lift it slightly during chapter transitions and the final "Beat the Paper" moment.

## 60-Second Voiceover

| Time | Chapter | Transcript |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00-0:07 | Intro | Welcome to the EST Lab. This is not four random revision jobs. It is a training run for the moment you open the paper and need a plan. |
| 0:07-0:17 | CORE | System one: CORE. This is what to say. CORE starts with the curriculum authority, then loads the assessed content: Initiative, Time Management, Finance, Applications, Communication, and Megatrends. |
| 0:17-0:27 | TERM | System two: TERM. This is the right language. The glossary document turns loose wording into Careers vocabulary, so the marker hears the terms they are listening for. |
| 0:27-0:37 | VTCS | System three: VTCS. Start with SCSA key words. Find the verb, topic, context, and structure before you write. Decode first. Answer second. |
| 0:37-0:47 | BOSS | System four: BOSS. Now the systems combine. Use content, precise terms, and the question decode to build an answer that can earn marks. |
| 0:47-1:00 | Beat the Paper | That is the loop: CORE, TERM, VTCS, BOSS. Train the systems, bank the evidence, lift your readiness, then beat the paper. |

## Lab Explainer Stingers

CORE: Load the knowledge from the curriculum authority. Pick a topic reactor, catch the key examples, then turn them into an answer.

TERM: Name it clearly from the glossary document. Match the term, lock the definition, and stop vague wording stealing marks.

VTCS: Read the SCSA key words like evidence: Verb, Topic, Context, Structure. The answer starts before the first sentence.

BOSS: Time to perform. Pull CORE, TERM, and VTCS together, then build the response that proves the training worked.

## CORE Topic Intro Lines

Initiative In Action: Spot the worker who steps in early, improves the situation, and helps the team before anyone has to ask.

Plan, Prioritise, Deliver: Put the urgent work first, use the right tool, and adjust the plan when the deadline starts moving.

Money Decisions Under Pressure: Track what is coming in, protect what must be paid, and seek reliable support before money stress takes over.

Build Stronger Applications: Target the role, match the criteria, and use STAR so your evidence is clear, specific, and convincing.

Communication That Moves Work Forward: Choose the right words, listen actively, and use body language that helps the message land.

Megatrends and Labour Market Information: Read the long-term signals, use labour market evidence, and explain how future work is changing.

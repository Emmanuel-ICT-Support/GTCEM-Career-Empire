# Career Empire World Visual Direction — Draft

## Why this document exists

`Career-Empire-Town-Rebuild-Brief.md` remains the source for curriculum structure, player progression, precincts and movement rules. It is not sufficient as visual direction. This draft fills the missing production decisions before future 3D assets are commissioned or generated.

This is a review draft. It does not approve changes to the live game.

## Recommended direction: ECC Career Journey Campus

Career Empire should feel like a believable Emmanuel-inspired learning campus that expands into a career precinct. A student begins in familiar school spaces and moves toward pathways, workplaces, enterprise and global opportunity. The progression must be visible in the built environment without turning the town into a generic city or a neon science-fiction world.

### The visual promise

* **Familiar first:** warm limestone or pale brick, verandas, shaded paths, courtyards, native planting and clear entries.
* **Career-facing next:** adaptable studio spaces, a town-square event space, a small workplace district and a pathway hub.
* **Future layer, used sparingly:** teal wayfinding, transparent learning panels, illuminated destination markers and visible community upgrades.
* **A lived-in place:** students, seating, gardens, bicycle parking, project displays and open learning spaces. Buildings are places to inhabit, not icons on grass.

## Art rules

| Element | Direction |
|---|---|
| Architecture | One shared campus family: limestone/pale brick, dark navy roofs or trims, generous shade, large windows and clearly framed entrances. |
| Landscape | Eucalypts, low native planting, sandstone edges, shaded seating and water-conscious gardens. Keep turf as a connecting surface, not an empty field. |
| Palette | Cream limestone, warm concrete, brick, ECC navy, teal/cyan for interaction, muted native greens. Reserve bright glow for active learning or progression. |
| Lighting | Clear Western Australian daylight with warm late-afternoon highlights; readable character faces and uniforms; no permanently dark character shading. |
| Digital layer | Helpful and restrained. Holograms explain a destination or achievement, never replace architecture or clutter every path. |
| Camera | Character-level walking view first; aerial view only for planning. Each destination needs an approach shot that frames its entrance and surrounding path. |
| Asset style | Premium stylised 3D with simplified, durable forms. Avoid mixing photorealistic buildings, low-poly trees and unrelated marketplace models. |

## Place hierarchy

1. **ECC arrival / Home and Identity:** the familiar starting point. It contains avatar, goals and personal wellbeing.
2. **Learning spine:** the walkable campus route containing EST Prep, careers, skills and pathways spaces.
3. **Civic heart:** one memorable square for orientation, class progress, celebrations and visible upgrades.
4. **Work and enterprise edge:** flexible workplace shells and interview/rights/safety experiences. It should look related to campus, only more contemporary and career-facing.
5. **Future horizon:** Megatrends and global links at the edge of the world. It becomes more expressive as students unlock change, not before.

## Concept routes considered

### A. ECC Career Campus — recommended

A compact, warm campus precinct with a central learning square. It makes ECC recognisable without attempting a literal copy. The digital layer is subtle and supports play.

**Best for:** an authentic public-facing concept, credible curriculum rooms, future ECC reference photography and an achievable first asset family.

### B. Campus-to-Career Corridor — combine with A

A single legible boulevard takes students from school learning spaces through a civic square toward career and workplace settings. This gives the player a physical journey that mirrors the curriculum journey.

**Best for:** walking gameplay, orientation and showing pathway progression.

### C. Future civic city — do not use as the base world

A dramatic neon city works for a Megatrends or Global Portal sequence, but it is too detached from ECC and risks making ordinary curriculum spaces feel like menus in disguise.

**Best use:** a contained later transformation or portal experience.

## Production gates before more assets

1. Approve the visual direction and a small palette/material board.
2. Design one **hero test slice**: ECC arrival, learning spine, civic heart and one EST room.
3. Define one modular building kit with: wall, window, roof, shade structure, entry, planter, sign and interior doorway pieces.
4. Create assets from that kit before adding isolated new buildings.
5. Test at walking height with a student avatar before promoting any asset to the live game.
6. Reserve real ECC photography for the hero ECC building and identifiable spaces once Marketing provides permission-cleared source images.

## ECC content opportunity

ECC can become the grounded learning campus within Career Empire: EST rooms, study support, Careers and Pathways, wellbeing, library/resource spaces and showcase rooms. The game should not claim to be a literal navigable copy of the school until reference material and permissions are settled. It can be an ECC-informed digital campus now, with a more faithful rendition introduced later as an approved precinct.

## Decisions needed next

* Confirm **ECC Career Campus + Campus-to-Career Corridor** as the direction.
* Identify 3–5 recognisable ECC features to carry into the future hero precinct once Marketing supplies images.
* Choose the first playable test slice: recommended **Arrival → EST Prep → Civic Heart**.

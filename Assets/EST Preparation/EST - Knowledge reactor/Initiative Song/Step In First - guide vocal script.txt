What is initiative? Step in, make it better.

I see the problem before it grows.
Spill on the floor, I do not walk past, no.
Low stock, long line, tools out of place.
I take action early, I pick up the pace.

I ask good questions, I look around.
Find a better way when work slows down.
Safer, faster, clearer too.
Improving the work is what I do.

Step in first, do not wait to be told.
Spot the chance, be brave, be bold.
Speak your ideas, help the crew.
Ask for more and learn what is new.

Act, improve, speak, support.
Step up strong in every sort.
That is initiative, clear to see.
Making work better for the team.

Proactive means I move ahead.
Not just doing what I am led.
Confidence, courage, empathy.
Motivation and curiosity.

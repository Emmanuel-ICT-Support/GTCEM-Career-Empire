Step In First - EST Initiative Song

Created as an original classroom-safe learning song for the Year 12 EST initiative content.

Recommended files:
- Step In First - full guide mix.mp3
  Use this first. It includes the backing track plus a spoken/chant guide vocal.

- Step In First - backing track.mp3
  Use this for students to perform or record their own vocal.

- Step In First - lyrics and chords.txt
  Lyric sheet, learning hook, and teaching cue.

Notes:
- The guide vocal is a computer-spoken chant layer, not a polished human sung vocal.
- The important retrieval hook is: Act, Improve, Speak, Support, Step Up.
- This maps to: being proactive, improving work practices, vocalising opinions, helping fellow workers, and seeking more responsibilities.

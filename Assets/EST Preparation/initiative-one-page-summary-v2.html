Enterprising behaviours

Initiative

Take action. Create impact.

Initiative means proactively taking action without being told. It includes spotting opportunities, suggesting improvements, helping others and taking on responsibilities that contribute positively to the workplace.

? What is initiative?

Being proactive, speaking up, helping others and taking responsibility to improve how work is done and support your team.

5 Five ways to show initiative

1

Being proactive

Anticipate problems or opportunities and act early.

2

Improving work practices

Suggest safer, easier or more efficient ways to complete tasks.

3

Vocalising opinions

Share ideas, feedback or concerns that could improve outcomes.

4

Helping fellow workers

Support colleagues so teamwork and productivity improve.

5

Seeking more responsibilities

Volunteer to learn new tasks or take on extra duties.

Across all five, strong workers ask questions and seek advice so their action is useful, safe and respectful.

! Initiative in action: examples

New worker

A warehouse assistant notices workers waste time searching for packing tools and suggests small supply stations at each bench.

Impact: equipment is nearby, efficiency improves and a new worker contributes a useful idea.

Junior manager

A retail supervisor reviews sales patterns and notices staffing does not match peak customer periods.

Impact: adjusted shift times improve customer service and workplace efficiency.

+ Initiative and teamwork

1

Helping colleagues - Step in when a co-worker is busy or struggling.

2

Sharing ideas - Suggest safer methods or more efficient processes.

3

Supporting goals - Take responsibility for tasks that help the whole team succeed.

4

Improving communication - Ask questions, share information and raise concerns.

5

Maintaining safe work - Identify hazards, clean spills and follow WHS practices.

Employers value initiative

It shows workers can notice what matters, act responsibly and improve how the workplace operates.

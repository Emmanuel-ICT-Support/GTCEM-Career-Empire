Every day, you make hundreds of decisions.
Most people never notice them.

The biggest decisions in your life rarely feel like big decisions.

So here is the question.
Do you actually have initiative?
Or do you wait until someone tells you what to do?

Nobody gets promoted because they waited.
Nobody builds a business by waiting.
Nobody changes their life by waiting.

But initiative is not just doing more.
And it is not always good.

The smartest people know when to act.
Why to act.
And how to act without making things worse.

Initiative is not a personality type.
It is a skill.
And skills can be trained.

The question is not, can you finish this module?
The question is, who could you become if you mastered it?

If you're tired of OneNote, PowerPoints, writing in your notebook, and group work, try Career Empire for a change of pace.

Career Empire is an online gaming experience that steps you through Year 12 Careers and Employability modules.

You earn while you learn, banking salaries you can use to upgrade your profile in the online store.

Everything you earn accrues tax, and that tax helps fund the class Community Fund.

You choose which parts of the community your class supports.

Then the Global Index shows how your class is going against other classes and other schools.

Think inter-school sport, Careers and Employability style.

You can request new shop items and give feedback on how to improve gameplay: what you're enjoying, and what we need to do better.

Just click the feedback button and have your say.

Best of all, Career Empire is built to grow your belief in you.

No matter what experiences you've had at school, at work, in life, or even in gameplay, you're building transferable skills every day.

Over time, you'll get better at articulating those skills and building your career profile as you go.

Career Empire: your portal to post-school life.

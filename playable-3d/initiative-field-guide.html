MY MARKET FIELD GUIDE · UNIT 3

Small actions. Useful change.

Initiative means taking constructive action without being told, using resourcefulness and problem solving. Respect your role, safety and the people affected.

Keep this field guide

At Mara’s stall

Phone customers have already paid; walk-up customers still need to order. Investigate before assuming. Suggest a supported trial, compare collection and packing, and explain the trade-off. Mara can discuss, approve or defer an idea.

Helping game Sam means offering suitable assistance and checking priorities. Moving him into a different role is not proof that you helped him. Asking for a new task includes agreeing training and limits.

Who could benefit?

Increased empowerment and recognition: trust, confidence and supported independence. Increased efficiency: fewer delays, errors or repeated tasks. To create work opportunities (for example, promotion): skills and a reputation for capability may open opportunities.

Explain effects for the worker, employer, colleagues and customers/clients. Benefits are possibilities, not guaranteed rewards. Carolan pp.28-31 · CEM12-U3-EB-03.d1-d3.

Put it away. Retrieve. Explain.

  1. List the five behaviours without looking.
  2. Describe an action, how you knew or learned to act and who was involved.
  3. Explain how/why the action could benefit someone; include a limit or trade-off.

Try again later with a different workplace. Matching a label is recognition; your own explanation needs separate feedback. Game practice does not prove workplace competence.
